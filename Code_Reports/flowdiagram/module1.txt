    %% Define Inputs
    Input[(Original Clean Dataset)] --> GenScript[Generation Script\ncorrupt_dataset_v3.py]

    %% Generation Stage
    GenScript --> ProbCheck{"Apply Corruption?"}

    ProbCheck -->|No| Mixed[Mixed Dataset\noutput_data]
    ProbCheck -->|Yes| CorruptType{"Failure Mode"}

    CorruptType -->|Saturation Boost| Sat[Deep Fried\nS-channel x2.0 to x4.0]
    CorruptType -->|Exposure Failure| Exp[Flash / Crushed\nMultiplier >2.5 or <0.3]
    CorruptType -->|Optical Defocus| Blur[Lens Blur\nGaussian Kernel 7x7 to 21x21]
    CorruptType -->|ISO Noise| Noise[Sensor Grain\nGaussian Noise]
    CorruptType -->|Sector Deletion| Del[Missing Views\nSparse Data]

    Sat --> Mixed
    Exp --> Mixed
    Blur --> Mixed
    Noise --> Mixed
    Del --> Mixed

    %% Detection Stage
    Mixed --> DetectScript[Screening Script]

    DetectScript --> Analyze{"Analyze Image Metrics"}

    Analyze -->|"Neon Clip > 5-10%"| RejectSat[Reject: Oversaturated]
    Analyze -->|"Histogram at 0 or 255"| RejectExp[Reject: Exposure Loss]
    Analyze -->|"MUSIQ Score < 40"| RejectBlur[Reject: Blurry / Noisy]

    Analyze -->|Passes all checks| Output[(Processed Clean Data\nprocessed_train)]

    %% Side Outputs
    DetectScript -.->|"--debug flag"| Visuals[Debug Visuals\nHeatmaps & Histograms]
